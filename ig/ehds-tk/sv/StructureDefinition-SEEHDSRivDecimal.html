Logisk modell: RIV-TA decimaltal

Officiell URL: https://fhir.inera.se/ig/ehds-tk/StructureDefinition/SEEHDSRivDecimal Version: 0.9.0
Draft per 2026-11-16 Beräkningsbart namn: SEEHDSRivDecimal

Decimaltal (xs:double/xs:decimal). Elementets värde ligger som textinnehåll i XML-elementet (representation xmlText).

Användningar:

  • Använd denna Logisk modell: GetMaternityMedicalHistory, GetMedicationHistory, GetObservations, RIV-TA PQIntervalType (clinicalprocess:activityprescription:actoutcome:2)... , RIV-TA PQIntervalType (clinicalprocess:healthcond:basic:1.2), RIV-TA PQIntervalType (clinicalprocess:healthcond:basic:2), RIV-TA PQType (clinicalprocess:activityprescription:actoutcome:1), RIV-TA PQType (clinicalprocess:activityprescription:actoutcome:2), RIV-TA PQType (clinicalprocess:healthcond:actoutcome:2), RIV-TA PQType (clinicalprocess:healthcond:actoutcome:3), RIV-TA PQType (clinicalprocess:healthcond:basic:1) and RIV-TA PQType (clinicalprocess:healthcond:basic:2)
